To use this module, you need to:

#. Go to *Expenses -> My Expenses -> Expenses to Submit* and create a new
   *Expense* with *Employee (to reimburse)* checked on the field *Paid By*.
#. Click on *Submit to Manager* button.
#. Click on *Approve* button.
#. Click on *Post Journal Entries* button.
#. (Optional) Click on *Register Payment* button, fill in the data of the
   wizard and click on *Validate* button.
#. After that, the *Expense report* will have an associated journal entry,
   optionally reconciled with a payment.
#. Click on *Reverse* button.
#. The *Expense report* will be set back to the *Approved* state. The original
   journal entry remains posted, a new reversal journal entry is created and
   reconciled with it, and any payment is cancelled (but not deleted). The
   fields *Reversed Move* and *Reversal Move* on the *Other Info* tab keep the
   traceability of the operation.

The same flow works for reports paid by the company
(*Paid By: Company*): the payment generated by Odoo is cancelled and the
expense entry is reversed, without removing any record.
